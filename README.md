# Kiber Savadlılıq (Cyber Literacy)

**Live site:** https://cyber-literacy.vercel.app

**Site language:** Azerbaijani 🇦🇿

## About

Cybersecurity awareness website for everyday people: seniors, parents and students with no technical background. It explains common online scams in plain language and shows what to do about them, without scaring people.

## Who it's for

- People who receive suspicious SMS, calls or WhatsApp messages
- Families who want to protect parents, grandparents and children online
- Anyone who wants practical habits instead of technical jargon

## What it covers

- Social media oversharing and how scammers use it
- Phishing, fake SMS, fake bank and courier calls
- Passwords, two-factor authentication, public Wi-Fi, online shopping
- What to do if your account is hacked
- Scam quiz, glossary and official institutions (CERT.GOV.AZ, police 102, Central Bank)

## Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Hosted on Vercel.

## Disclaimer

For informational and educational purposes only. If you lost money or data, contact your bank and the police (102) immediately.
